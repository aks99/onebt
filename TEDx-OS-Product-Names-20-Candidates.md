# Twenty name candidates for the product (31 October 2026)

**Brief from Ash:** self-explanatory, probably starting with T, sounding like it belongs near "TEDx", communicating the idea, and not infringing TED's trademarks. Category line: for idea-driven, legacy-driven speakers.

**The line that must not be crossed.** TED's license agreement bars its marks on any service offering guidance on giving or getting chosen for a TED or TEDx talk, and TED's Terms of Use forbid using "TED", "TEDx", "TED-Ed" and "Ideas Worth Spreading" without written authorization. So: no "TED" or "Ted" inside the name (TedPrep, Teddy, TEDready), no "x" suffix that copies the TEDx form (TalkX, StageX), no "Ideas Worth…" phrasing, no red three-letter wordmark in Helvetica. Starting with T and evoking talks and stages is fine; sounding like "TED" is not. Every name below still needs a clearance search (USPTO, India, UK, EU), a domain check and a social-handle check before use. Risk ratings are design judgments, not legal opinions.

## Shortlist and recommendation

1. **TalkOS** (closest to the working name, fastest to adopt, lowest risk).
2. **Talkworthy** (best alignment with "one idea worth a talk"; warmest).
3. **TalkStage** (plainest; strong for ads).
4. **Throughline** (best for a premium editorial brand; needs the most careful clearance).
5. **TalkLegacy** (best for the legacy-driven angle and the 45+ audience).

Suggested pairing: brand name **Talkworthy** with the product called **TalkOS** inside it ("Talkworthy, the TalkOS for idea-driven, legacy-driven speakers"), if both clear. Run both through clearance in week one; register the chosen name in the US, India, UK and EU in the software and education classes; secure the .com or .app and the LinkedIn, Instagram and YouTube handles before announcing.
