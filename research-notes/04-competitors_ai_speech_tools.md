# Competitive landscape: AI public-speaking practice, speech-analysis and presentation-coaching software (as of 30 Oct 2026)

Scope note: all vendor pages cited below were fetched on 2026-10-30 unless a different "viewed"/"captured" date is given. Third-party comparison articles are flagged where the publisher is itself a competitor (Orai, EchoPitch, SpeakUp Coach, Speakio, Orator, Outdoo all rank themselves). Web-search budget was exhausted partway through, so a handful of items (Google Interview Warmup status, Presentr/Vocalizer/Perfect Practice status, Yoodli ASR vendor, explicit recording-length caps) could not be chased down and are listed as gaps.

---

## Key Question 1: Competitor-by-competitor profiles (what it does, platform, metrics, feedback style, practice modes, pricing, free tier, target customer, traction, reviews)

### Takeaway
The category has split in 2025–26: the best-funded player (Yoodli, $40M Series B, >$300M valuation) has pivoted to enterprise sales/leadership roleplay and no longer leads with speech delivery for individuals; the only "live-meeting" consumer coach (Poised) shut down on 8 Oct 2026; and the consumer-facing survivors are mobile habit apps (Orai, Speeko, Wellspoken, Vocal Image) priced at $8–$30/month that optimise for 1–5 minute drills, not an 18-minute memorised talk. No tool found combines script structuring, long-form rehearsal, memorisation support and delivery analytics in one product.

#### Per-tool profiles

**Yoodli (Seattle; web-first)**
- What it does today: AI roleplays with customisable personas, multi-persona group roleplays, async "Coach Me" sessions built from an uploaded real call, a browser extension for real-time feedback during video calls, rubric scoring against Sandler/MEDDPICC/Challenger or custom rubrics, manager dashboards, 40+ languages, an MCP connector for CRM data — [Outdoo review, updated 17 Oct 2026 (competitor-published)](https://www.outdoo.ai/blog/yoodli-review-and-pricing).
- Public-speaking positioning: feedback on pacing, clarity, filler words, conciseness, structure, presence, tone and jargon; roleplays against "realistic AI audiences" including skeptical Q&A; audience settings (peers/technical/business); a "Yoodli Coach" weekly digest on how you came across in real meetings; sample speeches by Obama, Jobs and Kim Kardashian for reference; only named customer on the page is Knowmium — [yoodli.ai/use-cases/public-speaking](https://yoodli.ai/use-cases/public-speaking).
- Platform: web and browser extension; TechCrunch states "no dedicated mobile app" — [TechCrunch, 5 Dec 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/05/ex-googlers-yoodli-triples-valuation-to-300m-with-ai-built-to-assist-not-replace-people). (EchoPitch's table marks Yoodli as mobile; treat as unreliable — [EchoPitch](https://echopitch.io/best-public-speaking-app).)
- Funding/traction: $40M Series B led by WestBridge Capital with Neotribe and Madrona (Dec 2025); $13.7M Series A (May 2025); ~$60M total; valuation >$300M (tripled in six months); most revenue now enterprise; ARR up 900% in 12 months; ~40 employees; customers Google, Snowflake, Databricks, RingCentral, Sandler; coaching firms FranklinCovey and LHH run their methodologies on it; founders Varun Puri (ex-Google X) and Esha Joshi (ex-Apple), 2021 — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/05/ex-googlers-yoodli-triples-valuation-to-300m-with-ai-built-to-assist-not-replace-people). Earlier $6M seed (2022) — [Yoodli blog](https://yoodli.ai/blog/taking-yoodli-to-the-next-level-with-our-6m-fundraise).

An affiliate guide (updated 2 Oct 2026) claims the partnership has ended and quotes members saying Yoodli is "now just revoking it and trying to get us to pay" and "got what they wanted - access to loads of data from members in order to train and perfect its AI model" — [AI Tool Discovery](https://www.aitooldiscovery.com/guides/yoodli-ai-reddit). ## Key Question 6: Market size and growth signals (funding, user counts, analyst estimates)

### Takeaway
Two 2026 research reports size "public speaking training/coaching" at roughly $6.2–$6.4B (2025–26) growing ~6% to ~$11B by 2034–35, with online delivery the fastest segment; the clearest growth signals are Yoodli's $40M Series B at a >$300M valuation (Dec 2025), Wellspoken reaching ~$40K/month revenue within six months of launch, and Vocal Image/Wellspoken amassing tens of thousands of app ratings, against the counter-signal of Poised's shutdown.

### Cited Findings
- Public speaking training market: $6.20B (2025) → $10.84B (2034), 6.4% CAGR; online courses 34% share growing 8.4%/yr; virtual delivery fastest at 8.9%; in-person 45% share but 4.2% growth; corporate 43% of demand; North America 37%; named players include Yoodli, Orai, VirtualSpeech, Ultraspeaking, Toastmasters, Dale Carnegie; headwinds: flat club membership and free-content price pressure — [Dataintelo](https://dataintelo.com/report/public-speaking-training-market).
