## Key Question 1: TEDx coaching programs and done-for-you placement services — offers, prices, claims, guarantees, criticism, and TED's stance

### Takeaway
The "get on TEDx" market is a fragmented ladder running from $30 self-serve products to ~$15,000 done-for-you placement retainers, with the largest volume player (Thought-Leader/Leadr, Taylor Conroy) selling a $997 "30 Day Thought Leader" entry offer against a claimed $9,997 list price and a 60-day refund that is conditional on applying to 20 TEDx events. No legitimate vendor can guarantee selection; TED's own rules say speakers never pay to speak and that any service charging to "land you a TEDx talk" is illegitimate, and an October 2025 Dubai scam selling "guaranteed" slots at $10k–$25k ended with TED revoking the licence — so the ethical line in this market is between coaching/application help (tolerated, common) and paid slots (banned).

### Cited Findings

**TED's official position (primary source)**
- TED: "There is no central application to speak at a TEDx event through TED." Speakers are chosen independently by local volunteer TEDx teams; teams that haven't posted a call "are not accepting unsolicited pitches"; selection "is based on the idea a speaker is actively working on, not their story or credentials"; "Submitting multiple nominations for the same person does not increase the chance of selection." — [TED: Speaking at TEDx](https://www.ted.com/tedx/speaking-at-tedx) (fetched 30 Oct 2026)
- TED: "Speakers never pay to speak at a TEDx event." / "Speakers never pay, directly or indirectly." Speaker coaching is "provided free of charge" by events; "No TEDx speaker receives payment." Any service that charges to land you a TEDx talk is described as illegitimate; talks "connected to financial transactions are ineligible for publication"; paid offers should be reported to tedx@ted.com. — [TED: Speaking at TEDx](https://www.ted.com/tedx/speaking-at-tedx)
- TED's TEDx program page: "More than 4000 events are now held annually." — [TED TEDx program](https://www.ted.com/about/programs-initiatives/tedx-program)
- TED's live events directory showed "2,164 upcoming events" in the week of 26 Oct 2026, with status labels (Spaces available / Sold out / Invite only) and event types (Standard, Youth, Salon, University, TEDxPlay) but no indicator of which events accept speaker applications. — [TED TEDx events listing](https://www.ted.com/tedx/events)
- Organizer-side example: TEDxABQ opens applications every April, runs "Speaker Speed Dating" auditions (five 3-minute pitches + 3-minute Q&A), and requires ~12 weeks of coaching with at least 8 sessions — all run by the event. — [TEDxABQ Speak](https://www.tedxabq.com/speak/)

**Pay-to-play / "guaranteed TEDx" offers and TED's enforcement**
- Khaleej Times sting (4 Oct 2025): fraudsters posing as a Dubai TEDx event sold "Guaranteed TEDx Speaker Packages" — Spotlight $10,000, Elite $15,000, Ultimate $25,000 — plus a $3,000 advance via payment link; the article says the operators were Indian. TED's statement: "Our rules explicitly state that speakers are not to be charged or paid for their participation," and organisers charging speakers "may have their event licence removed and be subject to legal action." — [Khaleej Times sting](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/fake-tedx-scam-khaleej-times-sting-operation)
- Follow-up (7 Oct 2025): TED cancelled the event and revoked the organiser's licence after an internal investigation; organisers admitted a "serious mistake," returned to India, and refunds were under way; one professional had been quoted $15,000 for a "premium slot." A legitimate organiser (TEDx Happiness Street): "Scams like this erode public trust and hurt legitimate organisers." — [Khaleej Times follow-up](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/dubai-tedx-event-cancelled-alleged-25000-speaker-fees)

### Inferences
- Price architecture clusters into four bands: (1) $30–$100 self-serve guides (Str8ghtup, McDonald, Speaker Accelerator $48 masterclass); (2) $997–$4,997 courses/cohorts (Leadr 30DTL, TEDxCellence, UNMUTE, Big Talk Academy 2022, Moxie); (3) $10k–$15k high-touch or done-for-you (MIC $14,997; TSL VIP $14–15k; Mike Acker ~$10k); (4) $30k–$120k bespoke (Big Talk Speaker Salon, Brouk 1:1). A $100–$500/month software product sits in the empty gap between band 1 and band 2 and is cheaper annually than every coaching cohort.
- The only "guarantee" that survives TED's rules is effort-based (Leadr's refund requires 20 applications; MIC "pitch until selected"); no credible vendor guarantees selection, and the Dubai case shows "guaranteed slot" sellers get licences revoked.
- Leadr's claimed outcomes (400 → 650+ → 1,000+ stages) are self-reported, undated, and internally inconsistent across pages; none publish acceptance rates or denominators (clients enrolled vs. placed).
- The existence of a $25–50/hour freelance labor market for "TEDx application writing + status tracking" is direct evidence that application tracking and per-event tailoring are being done manually today — a software wedge.
- "Rolodex" products (Leadr's "Private TEDx Events Rolodex," MIC's "10 events per month" submissions, Leadr's "list of about 3,000 events") show that event-list access is a monetized bonus, not a commodity.

## Key Question 2: Speaker CRMs, marketplaces, directories, bureaus — pricing, features, and TEDx coverage

### Takeaway
Speaker platforms are cheap ($0–$850/year) directory-and-job-board products (SpeakerHub, SpeakerMatch, Innovation Women, Sessionize) or expensive implementation-led CRMs (SpeakerFlow: $3k–$10k setup + $99/user/month); none of the pages fetched list TEDx calls as a category or provide structured application tracking, and bureaus (BigSpeak/AAE) only take speakers already earning $10k+ per talk with 10+ paid gigs a year.
