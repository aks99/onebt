# Two-word .com names for the TEDx product: ranked shortlist (31 October 2026, round 4)

**Brief from Ash:** two English words put together, brandable, short, easy to remember, and instantly felt as related to TEDx without saying it. When you say it out loud to someone on the go, there must be only one way to spell it.

**What I checked:** 1,213 two-word names against Verisign's .com registry (RDAP), one at a time, 650 ms apart, on 31 Oct 2026. 190 are free, 1,023 are registered. Every name and its result is added to `rdap-checks-so-far.csv` (marked round 4) so nobody checks it again. Full ranked list with scores: `two-word-names-ranked.csv`.

**What is already gone:** almost every obvious pair. TalkStage, StageTalk, IdeaStage, TalkOS, StageOS, SpeakerOS, TalkReady, StageReady, TalkPath, StagePath, SpeakerPath, TalkMap, StageMap, TalkLab, StageLab, TalkCoach, StageCoach, TalkPrep, StagePrep, OnStage, GetOnStage, BigStage, MainStage, PageStage, BookStage, RedRug, RedCircle, OnTheDot, StageWings, TakeTheStage and OwnTheStage are all registered. So are nearly all Spark-, Story-, Voice- and Idea- pairs. What is still free sits one step away from the obvious: a less common second word (Runway, Wings, Formula, Method, Moment) or a short phrase (FindYourTalk, IdeaOnStage, EarnTheStage).

**How I kept the spelling safe.** I left out anything that can be written two ways when heard: homophones (write/right, hear/here, one/won, to/two/too, peak/peek, cue/queue), mic (people type 'mike'), number words (people type digits), the same letter on both sides of the join (StageEngine, ClapPath, SpeakerReady), an S sound running into an S (VoiceSpot), and centre/center. Names with a smaller spelling risk are kept low in the list and the risk is named.

**The trademark line.** None of these contains T-E-D, uses 'TEDx', or copies 'ideas worth spreading'. Every name still needs a clearance search (USPTO, IP India, UK IPO, EUIPO in classes 9, 41 and 42) and a handle check before you buy.

## Scoring (out of 100)

TEDx feel 30 (do people think 'stage talk' the moment they hear it), spelling certainty 25 (one way to write it from hearing), memorable and brandable 25, shortness 15. The total is scaled to 100.
