# Prestige and contrast .com names for the TEDx product: ranked shortlist (31 October 2026, round 5)

**Brief from Ash:** something with the feel of 'TEDWorthy', without TED in it. Names that make a speaker feel authoritative and carry power, prestige and contrast. Two-word, short, easy to remember, one way to spell it when said out loud.

**What I checked:** 584 names against Verisign's .com registry (RDAP), one at a time, 650 ms apart, on 31 Oct 2026. 275 are free, 309 are registered. Each name and its result is added to `rdap-checks-so-far.csv` (marked round 5). Full ranked list with scores: `prestige-names-ranked.csv`.

**What is already gone:** StageWorthy, TalkWorthy, IdeaWorthy, PodiumWorthy, KeynoteWorthy, QuoteWorthy, WorthHearing, WorthRepeating, SignatureTalk, SignatureStage, NameInLights, TopBilling, CommandTheRoom, CarryTheRoom, QuietPower, QuietRoar, SoftRoar, PinDrop, StageAuthority, TalkAuthority, AuthorityStage, AuthorityTalk, StageLegend, StageIcon, StageTitan, EliteStage, PrimeStage, GoldStage, CrownStage, RoyalStage, IconicStage, WorldStage and GlobalStage. The single-word prestige pairs are mostly taken. What is free is one step more specific: Headline-, Landmark-, Defining-, PinDrop-, RareAir-, MainStage- and BigStage- names, and the 'earned' and 'worthy' constructions.

**The trademark line.** None contains T-E-D. I also filtered out words that hide T-E-D, which rules out invited, selected, trusted, respected, celebrated and vetted. Nothing copies 'ideas worth spreading'. Two flags: Apple owns 'Keynote' for software, so the Keynote names carry real legal risk, and Hallmark is a brand, so I left the Hallmark names out of the ranking. Every name still needs a clearance search (USPTO, IP India, UK IPO, EUIPO in classes 9, 41 and 42) and a handle check.

**Spelling.** Same rule as round 4: no homophones, no number words, no repeated letter at the join. Caliber and calibre, honor and honour, tier and tear, and premier and premiere all split by country or by ear, so names built on them are left out of the ranking even where the domain is free. Smaller risks (prestige, gravitas, laurel, league, 'to') are scored down and named.

## Scoring (out of 100)

Authority and prestige 30, link to the stage 20, spelling certainty 25, memorable and brandable 15, shortness 10. Keynote names lose 6 points for the Apple risk.

## Three directions, and the best of each

- **Worthy and earned** (closest to 'TEDWorthy'): WorthyStage, StageEarned, WorthTheStage, DeserveTheStage, SpeakerWorthy, SpotlightWorthy.
- **Prestige and power**: HeadlineTalk, DefiningTalk, LandmarkTalk, TalkCrown, MainStageVoice, StagePrestige, GravitasTalk, RareAirTalk, CommandingVoice.
- **Contrast**: PinDropTalk (the smallest sound in the biggest room), StillRoar (stillness on stage, roar from the room), SmallVoiceBigStage, RoomFallsSilent, HeardByThousands, SoldOutTalk.
