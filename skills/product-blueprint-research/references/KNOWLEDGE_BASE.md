## 1. TEDx ecosystem (platform facts)

- Scale: TED's program page says "More than 4000 events are now held annually"; TED's Nov 2023 "State of the X" said 3,300+ events in 2023, 42,000+ since 2009, 180+ countries, 200,000+ talks; Feb 2025 materials said 3,000+ events and 250,000+ talks. Plan on 3,500 ± 500 per year.
- Directory (ted.com/tedx/events): 2,164 upcoming events on 30 Oct 2026; 50 per page; filters are search, upcoming/past, date, webcast; no API; no "accepting applications" flag; event pages at /tedx/events/{5-digit id}.
- 2026 listings (Dec 2025 snapshot of 1,365 events): US 306 (22%), India 190 (14%), Italy 69, UK 60, China 44, Canada 43, Türkiye 42, UAE 37, Brazil 37, Nigeria 26; Q1 62.4%, Q2 30.7%, Q3 5.1%, Q4 1.7%.
- TEDx Talks YouTube (26 Oct 2026): 44.7M subscribers, 272,588 videos, 9.16B views; mean ≈ 33,600 views per video; median not published.
- Featured on TED.com: about 1% historically (122 of 12,900 by 2012); "less than 0.1%" per TEDxBoulder; "about 100 per year" per one university report; one speaker reports a 15-month lag.
- License: free, one year, one event, one city, non-transferable; TED owns event names; 13 event types; tiers Community (≤100 attendees, $150 ticket cap), Community Plus (>100, requires attending a qualifying TED conference), Summit ($450 cap, after four 100+ events), Legacy (invite-only pilot).
- Selection: local volunteer teams; no central application; apply only via a public form on the event's TED.com page or official channels; one nomination per person; teams "rely on research, local networks, and interviews more than pitch forms"; "ideas, not profiles".
- Odds found: TEDxThirdWard 2026 786 applications → 155 semifinalists; TEDx Bard 2027 65+ → 15; TEDxBoulder top 30 interviewed → 12; TEDxCamden 300+ → 8; TEDxAlabaster 600+ applicants; coach guidance "5–10% per event", "apply to 5–10 events 6–8 months out"; one program claims "about 82 submissions" average before a first stage.
- Timelines: windows close 3–7 months before the event; decisions 2–8 weeks later; 6–12 weeks mandatory coaching; TED's prep calendar starts 6 months out and includes a 2-week break before dress rehearsals.
- Rejection reasons (organizers): "professional speaker/coach" framing; claiming to speak on anything; self-promotion ("blacklist"); mass pitches; no theme research; no audience action; mentioning prior TEDx talks; "signing up for courses or coaching that promise to help you land a TEDx talk" (TEDxDirigo).

## 2. TEDx rules that bind product behavior (quote where marked)

- Length: "typically under 18 minutes"; 3–9 minutes described as ideal (summary of TEDx Rules; re-verify wording); TED to organizers: "8–10 minutes is a sweet spot".
- Payment: "Speakers never pay, directly or indirectly"; events "may neither pay nor charge speakers"; "Fee-based 'TEDx services' are not legitimate. Talks tied to paid arrangements become ineligible for publication."
- Content: no selling ("if it feels like an advertisement, it probably is"); "Professional or circuit speakers, such as motivational speakers and life coaches, are not a fit"; TEDx "discourages curating talks from speakers who are actively seeking participation in multiple TEDx events"; no partisan politics; no proselytizing ("be wary of new age beliefs"); science must be testable and peer-reviewed; never medical advice.
- AI: "Never use AI as a substitute for fact-checking"; treat AI "like a tertiary source"; don't use generative AI with non-original input for slides.
- Slides: optional; one idea per slide; 1–2 lines; sans serif ≥42 pt (Speaker Guide); 16:9 1920×1080; no logos; videos ≤30 s (Anderson) and never over 60 s.
- Rights: Speaker Release gives TED exclusive distribution; speakers receive a link, not the file; no re-uploads, remixing or podcasts; speakers may post a 60-second clip for non-commercial social use if tagged to the official TEDx account (30 s for others); say "an independently organized TEDx event, not a TED event"; never use the TED or generic TEDx logo. TED's published content is CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 with the TED embed required; applicability to TEDx talks is not stated explicitly.
- Publication: organizers upload within one month; review 3–5 business days; TED "reserves the right to remove, reject, annotate or limit the distribution of any TEDx talk".
- Trademark: License Agreement §1 bars the marks "in connection with any work or service purporting to offer guidance on how to give an effective TED Talk or TEDx Talk, or how to get chosen to deliver a TED Talk or TEDx Talk"; public-speaking guidance allowed only if it "shall not in any way refer to TED". Terms of Use §7 (7 May 2024): no use of TED, TEDx, TED-Ed, Ideas Worth Spreading marks "for any purposes without prior written authorization". Terms §6.4 bans scraping and dataset/AI use absent a written license. Enforcement: C&D against "Drunk TED Talks"; Dubai license revoked Oct 2025 over $10,000–$25,000 speaker fees.
- TED's look (to stay away from): TED Red #EB0028 (Pantone 185) per TED's own TEDx logo page; the circulated #E62B1E (Pantone 485) is third-party; Helvetica or Inter Light; white or black backgrounds only.
- Organization: Oct 2025: TED stays nonprofit; Sal Khan "Vision Steward"; Logan McClure Davda CEO; Jay Herratti (ex-head of TEDx) to the board; handover at TED2026 (13–17 Apr 2026). No TEDx rule changes announced.

## 3. Talk craft numbers and frameworks (attributed)

- TED's idea definition: "more than a story or a list of facts; it draws a larger conclusion from evidence or observations"; criteria: local but relatable, defended by an argument (may have an antagonist), novel, changes perceptions, passes content guidelines.
- Anderson: throughline ≤ 15 words; "Who does this idea benefit?"; five tools (connection, narration, explanation, persuasion, revelation); "many of our best talks have been memorized word for word"; the half-memorized "valley of awkwardness"; eye contact with 5–6 people.
- Duarte sparkline: what is / what could be; call to action at the very end; "new bliss"; two ending points; 6-minute checkpoints; count-up then count-down timing.
- Gallo: 9 secrets grouped emotional / novel / memorable; message map with three messages.
- June Cohen: new idea or fresh angle; contagious emotion; "start strong" (TED cuts greetings); jargon test; one idea per talk.
- Pace: popular TED speakers 154–201 wpm (average 173); recommended presentation range 120–160; VirtualSpeech practice median 111; presentations rated 8+/10 had median 138. Word budgets at 130–160 wpm: 10 min ≈ 1,300–1,600 words; 18 min ≈ 2,340–2,880.
- Science of People (760 raters, 50 talks): top talks 465 gestures per 18 minutes vs 272; ≥240 gestures linked to high charisma; +30.5% vocal variety; smiling ≥14 s raised perceived intelligence; first 7 seconds predicted views; scripted-sounding delivery lowered charisma.
- Memory and nerves: Roediger & Karpicke 2006 one-week retention 56–61% with retrieval practice vs 40–42% with re-reading; Brooks 2014 "I am excited" beat "calm down" (karaoke accuracy ≈80% vs ≈53% anxious).
- Effort reported: 55 hours tracked; ~80 hours itemized; "hundreds of hours"; "one hour of rehearsal per minute of talk"; 60+ hours (TEDxDirigo); 3–5 hours/week (TEDxJacksonville).

## 7. Compliance facts

- Illinois BIPA (SB 2979, 2 Aug 2024): per-person accrual; $1,000 negligent / $5,000 reckless; electronic-signature consent valid; face geometry and voiceprints covered; publish a retention schedule.
- GDPR Art. 9: biometric data is special-category only "for the purpose of uniquely identifying a natural person"; explicit consent lifts the prohibition.
- India DPDP Rules notified 14 Nov 2025; core obligations ≈ May 2027; 72-hour breach report; erasure after one year of inactivity; verifiable parental consent under 18; penalties up to INR 250 crore.
- Scraping law (US): hiQ v. LinkedIn and Van Buren make CFAA claims over public pages weak; breach-of-contract claims viable where terms were accepted; Meta v. Bright Data (Jan 2024) narrow to Meta's terms.
