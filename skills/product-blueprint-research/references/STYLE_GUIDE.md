# STYLE GUIDE for blueprints and the product copy they contain

## Voice
Plain, warm, direct. Short paragraphs (1–3 sentences). Lead with the decision or the point, then the evidence. The reader is a busy owner or a builder; respect their time. The buyer of the product is the hero; the product is the pit crew.

## Punctuation and words
- No em dashes anywhere. Use commas, colons, periods, parentheses. En dashes are allowed only inside numeric ranges (3–7 months).
- No exclamation marks in document or product copy.
- Banned words and their cousins: unlock, unleash, elevate, demystify, delve, seamless, supercharge, game changer, revolutionize, groundbreaking, skyrocket, streamline, next level, new heights, say goodbye to, struggling to, let's dive in, here's the kicker, secret weapon, genuinely, honestly, straightforward.
- Never promise outcomes the product does not control (selection, income, views). Say "prepare", "find", "rehearse", "apply"; never "get selected", "guaranteed stage".
- Use the platform's own required phrasing where it exists (for example "an independently organized TEDx event", never "my TED talk" for a TEDx talk).

## Labelling facts
- Sourced fact: followed by a citation key in brackets [A12] or an inline source with date.
- Decision: "(decision)" or "decision for <owner>".
- Assumption: "(assumption; verify after 90 days)".
- Owner-stated fact: "owner-stated; confirm before public use".
- Vendor claim: "vendor claim, unverified".
- Fetch summary rather than verbatim: "as summarized; re-verify wording before quoting".

## Numbers, prices and dates
- Prices carry the date read ("viewed 30 Oct 2026") and the currency.
- Benchmarks carry the year and sample ("ChartMogul Feb 2026, n=200").
- Ranges beat false precision: "3–7 months" not "5.2 months".
- Conflicting figures: show both with dates, pick one, say why.
- Targets are labelled targets and tied to a benchmark.

## Citations
- In text: bracketed keys [A1, A2]. Keys must exist in the sources appendix.
- Appendix: numbered blocks grouped by topic; every entry has a title, URL and date; no unnumbered URL lists.
- Quote verbatim only inside quotation marks; paraphrase otherwise.

## Tables
Use tables for anything a builder will look up: features (ID, name, description, tier, phase, metering), tier matrix, credit costs, color tokens (token, hex, contrast), type scale, stack (layer, choice, why, price), data model, metrics (area, metric, target), risks (likelihood, impact, mitigation), sources.

## Headings and navigation
Numbered sections and subsections (1, 1.1). A reading map at the top. Cross-references by section number; check them after every edit.

## Product copy embedded in the document (microcopy, prompts, emails)
Same rules as above, plus: honest numbers over hype; rejection copy that normalizes and redirects; milestone copy that names the standard met; never name the product, a coach or a course inside anything the user sends to a third party.
